# Privacy Policy for Google Sheets Sidebar

**Draft for publication at:**  
<https://www.kevinsteachertech.com/google-sheets-sidebar/privacy>

**Effective date:** [INSERT EFFECTIVE DATE]

Google Sheets Sidebar (the “Add-on”) is a free educational resource published by
Kevin Cassidy under the name Kevin's Teacher Tech (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This
Privacy Policy explains what information the Add-on accesses, how that
information is used, and the choices available to users.

## Information the Add-on accesses

With a user's authorization, the Add-on accesses the Google spreadsheet in which
the user opens it. Depending on the feature selected, this may include:

- Sheet names and column headers;
- Cell selections and spreadsheet structure;
- Names, roster entries, grades, formulas, and other cell values in the current
  spreadsheet; and
- Values required to match rows and update columns between sheets (tabs) in the
  current spreadsheet.

The Add-on requests access to the current spreadsheet. It does not request broad
access to the user's Google Drive.

## How information is used

Spreadsheet information is used only to perform the action initiated by the
user, such as extending selected ranges, inserting grade formulas, or copying
matching values from a source sheet to the active sheet.

Names are compared without regard to capitalization or surrounding whitespace.
If the same name occurs more than once, those rows are paired by their order in
the source and destination rosters, and the Add-on reports the duplicate to the
user.

## Storage, sharing, and external transfer

The current version of the Add-on processes spreadsheet information within
Google Apps Script. It does not send spreadsheet content to a server operated by
Kevin's Teacher Tech, does not maintain an external database of spreadsheet
content, and does not sell or share spreadsheet content for advertising.

Google may process account and spreadsheet information as necessary to provide
Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, authorization, security, logging, and related
Google services. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's
applicable terms and privacy policies.

## Logging

Google Apps Script may create execution and error logs as part of operating and
diagnosing the Add-on. We do not intentionally place spreadsheet cell contents
or student records in application log messages. If a user sends a support email,
we receive the information that the user voluntarily includes in that message.

## Data retention and deletion

Because the current version does not maintain a developer-operated database,
there is ordinarily no separate copy of spreadsheet content for us to delete.
Updates made by the Add-on are stored in the user's spreadsheet and remain under
the control of that spreadsheet's owner and editors.

Users can revoke the Add-on's access through their Google Account permissions and
can uninstall it through Google Workspace Marketplace or Google Sheets. Users
may contact us at the address below with privacy or deletion questions.

## Security

We use the limited permissions reasonably necessary for the Add-on's functions
and rely on Google Apps Script to execute the Add-on. No system can be guaranteed
to be completely secure, and users should avoid sending sensitive spreadsheet
contents in support email unless necessary.

## Children's and student information

The Add-on may be used by educators with spreadsheets containing student
information. Schools and educators are responsible for determining whether use
of the Add-on is permitted by their institution and applicable privacy laws and
policies. The Add-on is not directed to children for the purpose of creating
accounts, and Kevin's Teacher Tech does not use the Add-on to create profiles of
students.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Add-on or applicable requirements
change. The published policy will show its effective date. Material changes to
data access or use will be described before or when the updated version takes
effect.
